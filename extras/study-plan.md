# How to Use This Book

For each question, speak the short answer aloud without looking for 45-90 seconds. Then compare it with the text, explain one trade-off in your own words and answer the follow-ups. Rebuild the example in a small project or SQL console when it is technical. Mark a question revised only when you can explain the edge case and a production implication. Prioritize missing understanding over memorizing phrasing. Questions beyond Q030 currently have plans only; use those as research prompts until their chapters are written.

# 7-Day Interview Preparation Plan

Plan for roughly 90-120 focused minutes each day: 35-45 minutes reading, 25-30 minutes answering aloud, 25-30 minutes hands-on work and 10 minutes reviewing misses. Adjust for your interview date.

The plan assigns every ID once as a primary day assignment; cross-day spoken practice is deliberate repetition. In the current partial manuscript, days 2-7 rely on the master plan rather than finished answers.
